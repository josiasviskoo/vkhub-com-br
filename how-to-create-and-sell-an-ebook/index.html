How to create an ebook to sell in 6 steps

Selling an ebook is one of the most accessible ways to start an online business: no stock, no shipping, and the same file can be sold as many times as you want. The hard part is knowing where to begin. The guide lays it out in this order:

  • Pick a niche: a specific problem people already pay to solve.
  • Plan and write: a chapter structure that keeps readers engaged.
  • Cover and formatting: the professional look that raises perceived value.
  • Sales platform: where to publish and get paid.
  • Marketing: strategies to land your first sales.
  • Automation: an email list and funnel that keep selling for you.
